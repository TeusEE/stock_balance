# Privacy Policy — Stock Balance

_Last updated: 2026-06-04_

Stock Balance ("the app") is a personal portfolio tracking and rebalancing
calculator. **The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal
information from its users.**

## Information we do NOT collect

- Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number, postal address)
- Account credentials of any kind
- Tracking identifiers (advertising ID, IDFA, IDFV used for tracking)
- Location, contacts, microphone, camera, photo library, or calendar data
- Usage analytics or crash reports that contain personal data

The app does not show advertising and does not integrate any third-party
analytics, attribution, or advertising SDK.

## Local data storage

All portfolio data you enter — account names, total amounts, holdings,
target percentages, current prices, owned share counts — is stored
**only on your device**, using iOS' standard `AsyncStorage`. This data
never leaves your device.

Uninstalling the app removes all stored data.

## External network requests

The app makes anonymous, unauthenticated HTTPS requests to **Yahoo
Finance's public chart endpoint** for the sole purpose of retrieving
public market data:

- `https://query2.finance.yahoo.com/v1/finance/search` — stock/ETF search
- `https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/{symbol}` — current
  price, currency, and historical daily close prices for the 6-month
  backtest feature

These requests are issued **directly from your device** to Yahoo's
servers. The app runs no backend of its own. The app does not send any
of your portfolio data to Yahoo or to any other party.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. The app does not
knowingly collect any data from children.
